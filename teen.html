How Funngro works

Become a teen earner in five steps

No CV, no interviews, no experience needed. Just your phone and a bit of effort.

  1. 1

    Download the app and sign up

    Get Funngro from the App Store or Google Play and create your free account.

  2. 2

    Set up your profile

    Add your interests and skills, and add the UPI ID or bank account where you want to receive payouts.

  3. 3

    Pick a brand campaign

    Browse live tasks — content, promotions, surveys, reviews — and choose one that suits you.

  4. 4

    Do the task and submit proof

    Follow the brief carefully, post or complete the task, and upload what the campaign asks for.

  5. 5

    Get approved, get paid

    Your submission is checked, and approved work is paid out by UPI or bank transfer.

Exact steps and requirements can vary by campaign. Always read the brief inside the app.

Benefits of practical experience

What early work experience gives you

Starting early isn't about pressure. It's about trying things, figuring out what you love and walking into the future more confident.

  • Accountability

    You learn what it means when someone depends on your work being done well and on time.

  • Teamwork & communication

    Working with brands teaches you how to understand instructions and ask the right questions.

  • Problem-solving

    Real tasks rarely go exactly to plan. Figuring it out is a skill that stays with you.

  • Career clarity

    Trying marketing, content or research early helps you discover what you actually enjoy.

  • Confidence

    Getting paid for your work by a real company is proof that your skills matter.

  • A stronger application

    Real projects give you specific stories to tell in college essays, CVs and interviews.
