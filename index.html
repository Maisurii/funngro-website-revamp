How it works

From brief to verified results in four steps

You bring the goal. Funngro handles the community, the coordination and the checking.

  1. 1

    Share your brief

    Tell the Funngro team what you want to achieve — awareness, trials, content, installs or insights — plus your audience and timeline.

  2. 2

    Shape the campaign

    Together you pick the format, the actions you'll pay for and the guidelines earners must follow.

  3. 3

    Young earners take part

    The campaign goes live in the Funngro app, where young people choose it, complete the task and submit proof.

  4. 4

    Get verified results

    Submissions are checked before they count, earners are paid via UPI, and you receive the outcomes and content.
